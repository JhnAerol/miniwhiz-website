Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 30, 2026 • Last Updated: August 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to MiniWhiz ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and the privacy of your children. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website services, including Google OAuth authentication.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to operate the application, manage parent accounts, and customize learning experiences for children:

  • Parent Account Credentials: Name, email address, and encrypted authentication tokens provided via sign-up or Google OAuth single sign-on.
  • Child Profile Data: First name or nickname, selected cartoon avatar, and learning progress indicators (e.g., stars earned, story completion scores).
  • Device & Diagnostics Data: Device model, OS version, application crash logs, and performance metrics.

3. Use of Google OAuth Data

When you sign in using Google OAuth, MiniWhiz requests basic profile access (your name, email address, and profile picture). We use this information solely to create and authenticate your account. We do not sell, rent, or share your Google account data with third parties or advertisers.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

MiniWhiz complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. All child profiles are created and managed exclusively by parents or guardians under parent PIN protection.

5. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/SSL) for data transmission and secure cloud storage for database persistence. Account credentials and parent security PINs are protected using cryptographic hashing.

6. Your Data Rights & Deletion Requests

Parents have the right to review, modify, or permanently delete their account and associated child profile data at any time through the Parent Control screen in the application or by contacting our privacy team.
